## 1. Institute Identity

**Source:** https://nightingalennmi.com/ , https://nightingalennmi.com/about/

- Full name: "Nightingale Nursing and Midwifery Institute (NNMI)"
- Institute is stated to be named after Florence Nightingale, "pioneer of modern nursing" (About page).
- Tagline/motto (used on homepage and About page): "Enter to Learn, Leave to Serve"
- Mission (homepage phrasing): "Empowering future nurses with real skills" / "Learn, train, and grow with confidence"
- Mission (About page, fuller statement): "Deliver high-quality nursing and midwifery education that prepares students for success both in Bangladesh and internationally."
- Vision (About page): "To become a Center of Excellence in nursing and midwifery education, raising the standards of healthcare in Bangladesh and empowering students to shine on the global stage."
- Affiliations/recognitions claimed:
  - "Approved by the Ministry of Health & Family Welfare and Bangladesh Nursing & Midwifery Council" (homepage)
  - "Approved by the Ministry of Health and Family Wellness" and "Approved by the Bangladesh Nursing and Midwifery Council" (About page — note slightly different wording of the ministry name than the homepage)
  - "Associate of the Institute of Allergy and Clinical Immunology of Bangladesh (IACIB)" (About page)
  - "Specialized training from South Korean medical experts" (About page)
  - "National Skill Development Authority (NSDA)" approval is claimed on the Message from MD page (see §3) for the Caregiver course specifically — not repeated on the About page.
- The About page contains a statistics/counter block (happy students, successful nurses, years of experience, professional teachers) but all values currently render as "0" — i.e., no real numbers are actually published there.

## 2. Courses Offered

The site currently advertises exactly two formal diploma programs (plus a short course mentioned only in the MD's message — see §3). No B.Sc Nursing, Post Basic B.Sc Nursing, GNM, or ANM programs are offered/advertised as current programs (B.Sc/Post Basic B.Sc/M.Sc Nursing are mentioned only as external "higher education pathways" graduates might pursue elsewhere — see §7).

### 2a. Diploma in Midwifery
**Source:** https://nightingalennmi.com/diploma-in-midwifery/

- Duration: "3-year program" with an included "6-month internship"
- Intake: "40 (Forty) per year", currently in its "5th batch"
- Eligibility: "SSC and HSC or equivalent (any group; science preferred)"; "Minimum GPA as per BNMC guidelines"
- Fees: Admission fee (one-time) BDT 50,000; Hostel fee (monthly) BDT 2,500; total 3-year cost stated only as "As per enclosed budget" (no figure given on-page)
- Curriculum listed by year:
  - Year 1: Anatomy, Physiology, Embryology, Fundamentals of Midwifery, Community Midwifery, Basic Nursing Skills, Nutrition, English & Communication
  - Year 2: Antenatal Care, Intranatal Care, Postnatal Care, Neonatal Care, Pharmacology, Obstetric Emergencies
  - Year 3: Complicated Pregnancy & Labor, Family Planning, Community Midwifery, Ethics & Professionalism, Health Education, Research Methodology
- Regulatory approval stated: "Approved by the Bangladesh Nursing and Midwifery Council (BNMC) and the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare."
- Facilities note on this page: "Safe and secure female hostel accommodation" with access to labs, library, computer facilities.

### 2b. Diploma in Nursing Science & Midwifery
**Source:** https://nightingalennmi.com/diploma-in-nursing-science-midwifery/

- Duration: "3 Years" (page separately references "Over 3.5 years" when describing total course fee coverage — this internal inconsistency is present on the source page itself)
- Intake: "Total Seats: 45 (Forty-five) per year"
- Eligibility: "SSC and HSC or equivalent (Science group preferred)"; GPA per BNMC guidelines
- Fees: Admission fee BDT 50,000 (one-time); Hostel fee BDT 2,500/month; total course fee described only as "per enclosed budget" (no figure given); additional government exam fees and internship costs noted; "Monthly or quarterly options available" for installments
- Curriculum listed by year:
  - Year 1: Basic sciences (anatomy, physiology, microbiology), nursing fundamentals, community health nursing
  - Year 2: Medical nursing, surgical nursing, pediatric nursing, nursing informatics
  - Year 3: Midwifery, mental health nursing, nursing management, research and statistics
- Approval: "Bangladesh Nursing and Midwifery Council (BNMC)" and Ministry of Health and Family Welfare
- Clinical training: placements at Dhaka Medical College Hospital and Suhrawardy Medical College Hospital

## 3. Admission Process

**Sources:** https://nightingalennmi.com/admission/ , https://nightingalennmi.com/admission-form/ , https://nightingalennmi.com/message-from-md/

- Eligibility: "Must have passed SSC and HSC (or equivalent) examinations"; science group preferred; GPA per BNMC guidelines. "All regions welcome; special consideration for female students from Chittagong Hill Tracts and underprivileged backgrounds."
- Required documents:
  - Attested photocopy of SSC and HSC certificates and mark sheets
  - Four recent passport-size color photographs
  - Attested photocopy of Birth Certificate or National ID
  - "Additional documents as required by BNMC or Ministry of Health"
- Procedure: "Visit the institute or contact the admission team for application procedures," or use the online admission form (link above).
- No specific application deadlines or intake dates are published anywhere on the site.
- Fees (labeled "Session 2024-2025" on the Admission page):
  - One-time admission fee: BDT 50,000
  - Monthly hostel fee: BDT 2,500
  - Payable via monthly or quarterly installments
  - Stated to cover tuition, hostel, meals, lab work, clinical training, uniforms, books, exams, and internship
- Program summary repeated on this page: Diploma in Nursing Science & Midwifery — 3 years, 45 seats/year, Batch 5; Diploma in Midwifery — 3 years, 40 seats/year, Batch 5. (Note: the individual course pages, §2a, say Diploma in Midwifery is in its "5th batch," matching here; both programs are listed as Batch 5.)
- Admission Form fields collected: Full Name, Mother's Name, Date of Birth, Gender, Blood Group, Nationality, Religion, Marital Status, Present Address, Permanent Address, Student Phone, Guardian Phone, Email, optional Payment Address, SSC Result (1st/2nd/3rd division fields), HSC Result (same), choice of course (Diploma in Nursing Science & Midwifery or Diploma in Midwifery), Payment method (Full/Monthly/Quarterly), hostel requirement (Yes/No), and an open-ended "Why you are choosing NNMI" question. The form itself states no deadlines, fees, or admission instructions.
- The Message from MD page additionally mentions a "3-month Caregiver course paired with a 6-month Japanese language training" — this is not listed as a formal program anywhere else on the site (not on the nav, not on Admission, not with its own fee/eligibility page).

## 4. Facilities

**Source:** https://nightingalennmi.com/our-facilities/ (cross-referenced with homepage summary)

- Classrooms: "5 large, well-ventilated classrooms" with multimedia projectors, whiteboards, sound systems, comfortable seating. (Homepage calls these "5 Well-equipped Multimedia Classrooms.")
- Laboratories — "7 Specialized Labs" total, named individually:
  1. Fundamental Lab (basic nursing skills practice)
  2. Anatomy & Physiology Lab (skeletal models, organ models, charts)
  3. Microbiology Lab (microscopes and culture tools)
  4. Nutrition Lab (therapeutic diet planning with food models)
  5. Midwifery Lab (delivery models, pelvic models, neonatal care kits)
  6. Pharmacology Lab (drug types, dosage, administration training)
  7. Computer & Audio-Visual Lab (nursing informatics and computer training)
- Library: textbooks, reference books, local and international journals, described as a "study-friendly environment for silent reading."
- English Language Club: spoken English, writing, public speaking.
- Co-curricular: sports competitions, cultural programs, seminars, science fairs.
- No mention anywhere on the site of a dedicated transport/bus service, sports ground/gymnasium facility, or a separate cafeteria/canteen page — these are simply absent from the content, not confirmed one way or the other beyond what's stated.
